FIG 02

Cellular Level: How a Single Neuron Sends a Signal

  1. 01
    Dendrites (Inputs): Branching receivers that pick up chemical signals from neighboring neurons.
  2. 02
    Cell Body (Decision): Sums all incoming signals. If the total crosses a threshold, the neuron fires.
  3. 03
    Axon & Myelin (Wire): An electrical pulse (~0.1 V inside the cell) races down the axon. Fatty myelin wraps speed it up.
  4. 04
    Synapse (Handoff): At the end, the pulse triggers tiny chemical packets that cross the microscopic gap to the next neuron.
